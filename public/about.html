Eye-Opening Facts Behind Everyday Consumables

⚠️ Kitchen Sponges (Recommended: 2 Weeks)

A kitchen sponge used for two weeks reaches a higher bacterial density than a toilet seat. Replacing it every two weeks prevents spreading bacteria onto clean dishes.

⚠️ Toothbrushes (Recommended: 1 Month)

Even slightly splayed bristles reduce plaque removal efficiency by nearly 40%. Because bacteria thrive at the damp base of bristles, dental associations recommend monthly replacement.

Water filter cartridges, electric toothbrush heads, razor blades, range hood filters, and refrigerator ice maker filters are just a few of the many everyday items whose hygiene and performance degrade without regular replacement.

Because replacement intervals vary from days and weeks to months or years, it is easy to forget when an item was last changed—or realize you are out of spares right when you need one.

Replenish centralizes the lifecycle of all your household consumables, automatically calculating both the next replacement date and your advance restock reminder date.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q Is Replenish completely free to use?

Yes, Replenish is 100% free. Item registration, automatic due-date calculation, hygiene guides, calendar view, history logs, and CSV export are all available at no cost.

Q Do I need to register an account or log in?

No. You can use every feature immediately in your browser without entering an email address, password, or creating an account.

Q Is my registered data ever sent to an external server?

Never. Replenish stores all registered data strictly inside your browser's local storage (LocalStorage) on your device.

Q Can I install Replenish on my smartphone home screen?

Yes! Replenish supports PWA installation. Use "Add to Home Screen" in iOS Safari or "Install App" in Android Chrome to launch it full-screen like a native app.

Q Can I transfer my data when switching to a new phone?

Yes. From the Data Management menu, you can export your registered items and replacement history as a CSV file and import it on your new device.

Q How are the recommended replacement cycles in the guides determined?

They are based on official manufacturer manuals, dental and public health association guidelines, and peer-reviewed microbiological research, balancing household hygiene and durability.
