2. Where Your Data Is Stored (100% Local Storage)

Safe, Private On-Device Storage by Design:
All consumable item information registered in the Service (including item names, model numbers, replacement cycles, replacement history logs, and notes) is stored exclusively inside your web browser's LocalStorage on your device (key: replenish_consumable_items_en_v1).
